Tier list

Rating is an overall (OVR) like in sports games: 75 is an average game in that lobby, 85+ a great game, 90+ took over the lobby, 99 the max (built from score 40%, kills 30%, K/D 15%, objective 15%, plus a bonus for a win). Tiers are on a curve within each leaderboard: top 1 in 8 is S, then a quarter each A, B and C, and the rest D (ties share a tier). SR starts at 1000 each season and moves with every result. Levels 1–55, then prestige.
